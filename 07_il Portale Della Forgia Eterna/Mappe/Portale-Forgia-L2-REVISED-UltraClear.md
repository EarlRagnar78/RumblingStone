### 🎯 TATTICA ATTRAVERSAMENTO:

**OPZIONE A - BRIDGES (Safest but Guarded):**
- Cross Ponte Nord (Riga 10) → East Platform
- Move south on East → Cross Ponte Centro (Riga 27) → West
- Move south on West → Cross Ponte Sud (Riga 44) → East
- Exit Riga 53 East side
- Encounters: FE2 (under bridge), FE3 (patrol east)

**OPZIONE B - STRAIGHT WEST (Fast but Hot):**
- Stay West Platform full 80m
- Avoid bridges = avoid FE2
- Risk: FE1 patrol blocks path (combat inevitable)
- Heat zones: Persistent 1d6/round (Fort CD 15)

**OPZIONE C - LEAP LAVA (Risky but Direct):**
- *Jump* spell: 30ft (9m) can clear lava width (10.5m barely!)
- *Fly* spell: Trivializes encounter (40ft/round = 12m)
- *Levitate*: Slow (6m/round) but safe
- Risk: Dispel in mid-air = instant death lava
