## Spell Mechanics

### Casting a Spell
1. Choose spell (prepared or known)
2. Determine slot level (apply metamagic if any)
3. Pay components (V, S, M, F, DF, XP)
4. Resolve any Concentration checks (if threatened)
5. Apply to target; target makes saving throw if allowed
6. Apply spell resistance if applicable

### Spell Save DCs
**DC = 10 + spell level + ability modifier (INT for Wizard, WIS for Cleric/Druid/Paladin/Ranger, CHA for Bard/Sorcerer)**

### Spell Resistance (SR)
Caster must roll d20 + caster level ≥ SR to overcome.
Certain spells are SR: No (always bypass).

### Concentration
Distraction while casting → Concentration check DC 10 + damage taken (if hit) + spell level
Defensive casting: DC 15 + spell level

## Spell Descriptors

**Saving Throw Types**: Fort, Ref, Will; result: Negates / Half / Partial / Harmless / None

**Spell Ranges**:
| Range | Distance |
|---|---|
| Personal | Caster only |
| Touch | Melee touch attack |
| Close | 25 ft + 5 ft/2 levels |
| Medium | 100 ft + 10 ft/level |
| Long | 400 ft + 40 ft/level |
| Unlimited | Same plane |

**Duration**:
- Instantaneous: Effect happens and ends (no dispel)
- Concentration: Lasts while caster concentrates (standard action each round)
- Fixed: Rounds, minutes, hours, days per level
- Permanent (D): Lasts until dispelled

## Key Spells by Level (SRD Core)

### Cantrips / 0-Level
- **Detect Magic**: detect magical auras in 60-ft cone; concentration up to 1 min/level
- **Light**: touch; 20-ft radius bright light, 20-ft shadowy beyond; 10 min/level
- **Mage Hand**: telekinesis, 5 lb limit, close range
- **Prestidigitation**: minor magical effects, 1 hr
- **Read Magic**: decipher magical writing

### 1st Level
- **Alarm**: ward area; mental or audible alert when triggered; 2 hrs/level
- **Burning Hands**: 15-ft cone; 1d4/level fire (max 5d4); Ref half
- **Charm Person**: humanoid regards caster as friendly; Will negates
- **Color Spray**: blind/stun/unconscious by HD vs. caster level; Will negates
- **Cure Light Wounds**: 1d8+1/level (max +5) healing
- **Magic Missile**: 1 missile +1 per 2 levels above 1st (max 5 at CL 9); 1d4+1 force each; no save; SR applies
- **Shield**: invisible shield; +4 AC; blocks magic missiles; 1 min/level
- **Sleep**: 4 HD of creatures sleep (lowest HD first); Will negates; 1-round casting time

### 2nd Level
- **Bull's Strength**: +4 STR; 1 min/level
- **Darkness**: 20-ft radius of darkness; 10 min/level
- **Hold Person**: paralyze humanoid; Will negates; 1 round/level
- **Invisibility**: invisible until attack or cast; 1 min/level
- **Knock**: open locked/stuck/held/barred door; ignores arcane lock up to 3rd
- **Mirror Image**: 1d4+1/3 levels images; absorb hits instead of caster
- **Scorching Ray**: 1 ray (+1/4 levels above 3rd, max 3); 4d6 fire; ranged touch

### 3rd Level
- **Dispel Magic**: cancel magic; max +10 caster level check (3.5 errata: +10 cap)
- **Fireball**: 20-ft radius; 1d6/level fire (max 10d6); Ref half
- **Fly**: 60-ft fly speed, good maneuverability; 1 min/level
- **Haste**: +1 attack on full attack; +1 AC dodge; +1 Ref; +30-ft speed; 1 round/level
- **Lightning Bolt**: 120-ft line; 1d6/level (max 10d6); Ref half
- **Slow**: negate haste; −1 attack; −1 AC dodge; −1 Ref; half speed; 1 round/level

### 4th Level
- **Confusion**: 1 creature/level; confused condition; Will negates
- **Dimension Door**: teleport self (+carried) up to long range; no error
- **Enervation**: 1d4 negative levels; ranged touch; no save; SR Yes
- **Greater Invisibility**: invisible even while attacking; 1 round/level
- **Ice Storm**: 20-ft radius; 3d6 bludgeoning + 2d6 cold; no save; 1 round

### 5th Level
- **Cone of Cold**: 60-ft cone; 1d6/level cold (max 15d6); Ref half
- **Dominate Person**: humanoid fully controlled; Will negates; permanent until dispelled
- **Hold Monster**: as Hold Person but any creature
- **Teleport**: teleport up to 100 miles/level; possible error table
- **Wall of Force**: invisible wall; impervious to most attacks; cannot be dispelled

### 6th Level
- **Chain Lightning**, primary: 1d6/level; secondary: ½ damage; Ref half
- **Contingency**: sets spell to trigger when condition met; 1 day/level
- **Disintegrate**: 2d6/level damage (Fort partial = 5d6); destroys up to 10 cu ft of matter
- **Globe of Invulnerability**: blocks ≤4th-level spells; 1 round/level
- **True Seeing**: see through illusions, invisible, polymorphed, etc.; 1 min/level

### 7th Level
- **Finger of Death**, slays one living creature; Fort partial: on a successful save instead takes 3d6 +1/level (max +25)
- **Limited Wish**: duplicates most spells ≤6th level; flexible
- **Reverse Gravity**: objects/creatures fall upward; 1 round/level
- **Spell Turning**: reflect 1d4+6 levels of spells back; 10 min/level

### 8th Level
- **Horrid Wilting**: 1d6/level (max 20d6) desiccation; plants/water creatures take d8; Fort half
- **Incendiary Cloud**: 20-ft radius cloud; 4d6/round fire; Ref half; lingers 1 round/level
- **Mind Blank**: immune to divination, mental effects; 24 hours
- **Power Word Stun**: stun creature with ≤150 HP; no save

### 9th Level
- **Gate**: planar portal or call specific outsider
- **Imprisonment**: entomb creature; no save; SR Yes
- **Meteor Swarm**: 4 meteors; 2d6 bludgeon + 6d6 fire each; Ref half
- **Power Word Kill**: kill creature with ≤100 HP; no save; SR Yes
- **Time Stop**: 1d4+1 free rounds; others frozen
- **Wish**: alter reality; limited only by GM discretion; 5,000 XP

## Metamagic Reference

| Feat | Spell Level Increase | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Empower Spell | +2 | ×1.5 all variable numeric values |
| Enlarge Spell | +1 | Double range |
| Extend Spell | +1 | Double duration |
| Heighten Spell | +variable | Treat as higher level (affects DCs) |
| Maximize Spell | +3 | All variables at maximum |
| Quicken Spell | +4 | Cast as swift action |
| Silent Spell | +1 | No verbal component |
| Still Spell | +1 | No somatic component |
| Widen Spell | +3 | Double area |

**Sorcerers/Bards with metamagic**: Must use a full-round action (not standard) to apply metamagic spontaneously.
**Prepared casters** (Wizard, Cleric, etc.): Prepare spell in higher slot in advance; no action cost at casting.

## Spell Interaction Rules

**Stacking**: Same spell from same source does NOT stack. Different spells DO stack unless same type (e.g., enhancement bonuses don't stack with enhancement bonuses).

**Dispel Magic**: Dispel check = d20 + caster level (max +10) vs. DC 11 + spell's caster level.

**Antimagic Field**: Suppresses all magic within; spells fail; magic items mundane; summoned creatures vanish.

**Dead Magic Zone**: As antimagic but permanent, nonmagical, and cannot be dispelled.

## Liste di classe — l'ancora delle liste del generatore

Questa sezione esiste per una ragione sola, e va detta o fra sei mesi qualcuno la
tratta come un doppione da cancellare: `scripts/genera_creatura.py` sceglie gli
incantesimi di un PNG generato, e **la scelta va controllata contro qualcosa
scritto altrove**. Un test che confrontasse le liste del generatore con una
costante Python accanto non troverebbe niente.

La divisione del lavoro fra i due file è netta, ed è quello che rende il
controllo utile invece che circolare:

| Dove | Cosa dice | Che genere di cosa è |
|---|---|---|
| **questo file** | *quali incantesimi quella classe può lanciare, a che livello* | un fatto di regole, dal SRD |
| `genera_creatura.py` | *quali di quelli un controllore sceglie davvero* | una scelta di progetto |

Il test verifica un'inclusione: ogni incantesimo scelto dal generatore deve
comparire qui, per **quella** classe e **quel** livello. È così che si accorge di
un druido con la *palla di fuoco* — che è precisamente il difetto per cui il
lotto I del piano esiste.

Le liste sotto sono **selezioni**, non le liste complete del SRD: prendono gli
incantesimi che un PNG da incontro lancia davvero. Il livello è quello della
classe indicata, e **lo stesso incantesimo sta a livelli diversi per classi
diverse** — *dissolvi magie* è 3° per mago, chierico e bardo e 4° per il druido,
e leggerlo dalla riga sbagliata è esattamente l'errore che il test cerca.
